What Wavy adds

You sign in with your normal YouTube / Google account. Everything else happens locally on your device – and every extra can be switched on and off.

10-band equalizer

Presets, your own profiles and a separate EQ for headphones and speakers that switches automatically.

Karaoke lyrics

Synced lyrics in full screen, highlighted word by word – or as an overlay on top of games and other apps.

Dynamic Island

A small player at the top of your screen with cover, controls and volume.

Live wallpaper

A wave, bars or particles moving to the music behind your desktop icons, optionally with the current lyric line.

Friends & listen together

See what friends are listening to, send songs and share a room code so everyone hears the same thing in sync.

Your own look

Themes, accent color from the album cover, backgrounds in cover colors and a glowing wave that pulses to the beat.

Sound effects

Bass boost, reverb, 8D, slowed + reverb, nightcore and volume normalization.

Discord & Last.fm

Shows what you are listening to as your Discord status and scrobbles to Last.fm.

RGB to the beat

Your lighting follows the music: OpenRGB (Corsair, ASUS, MSI …), Razer Chroma, WLED, Hue and Govee.

Appearance

Looks the way you want

Wavy takes its colors from the song that is playing. Pick a theme, an accent color or your own background image – the player bar, the wave and the menus follow along.

Sound

An equalizer for YouTube Music

Ten bands from 32 Hz to 16 kHz, a preamp and presets from Bass Boost to Vocal. Save your own profiles and let Wavy switch between headphones and speakers on its own.

Dynamic Island

Always in view, never in the way

The Dynamic Island shows the current song at the top of your screen and expands when you hover over it. Play, skip, like and change the volume without opening the app.

First start on a Mac

Wavy is not notarized by Apple (that costs $99 a year). On the first start macOS therefore says “Wavy is damaged and can’t be opened”. The app is fine – this takes 30 seconds:

  1. Open the DMG and drag Wavy onto Applications.
  2. If macOS says “damaged”, click Cancel (not “Move to Trash”).
  3. Open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type “Terminal”), paste this line and press Enter:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Wavy.app && codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/Wavy.app
  4. Open Wavy again – from now on it starts normally.

The command removes the download lock macOS puts on apps that are not registered with Apple and signs Wavy locally on your Mac.

Questions & answers

Is Wavy free?

Yes. Wavy is free on every platform, without ads from Wavy and without a subscription. You sign in with your normal YouTube / Google account.

Is Wavy an official YouTube Music app?

No. Wavy is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. It shows the normal YouTube Music website in its own window and adds features on top.

Which systems does Wavy run on?

Windows 10 and 11, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux (AppImage, .deb, .rpm, .tar.gz), Android 8 or newer and iPhone via SideStore.

Does Wavy collect my data?

No. There is no tracking and there are no advertising IDs. Settings, stats and history stay on your device. Wavy only connects to YouTube Music and to services you switch on yourself, such as lyrics, Last.fm, Discord or friends.

Why does Windows or macOS show a warning?

Wavy is not signed with a paid certificate (Windows) and not notarized by Apple (Mac). On Windows click “More info → Run anyway”; on a Mac follow the short guide above. Every release is also checked on VirusTotal – the results are listed on the release page.

How does Wavy update?

Wavy checks for a new version at startup and every few hours. Click “Update now”, Wavy restarts and your settings stay exactly as they are.

Which languages does Wavy support?

English and German. Wavy follows your system language and you can switch it in the settings.
